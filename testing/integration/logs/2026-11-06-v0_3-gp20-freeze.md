## Conclusion and next experiment

Motor activation acceptance is **not passed**. The record establishes a reporting stall associated with the first request; it does not establish the processor's exact state, failure instruction, reset/brownout, deadlock, timer failure or a host-only issue. A displayed COM port is not proof that the CPU/tasks continue running; absence of visible movement does not prove no electrical drive occurred.

Review traced the fresh-press controller through motor_pulse_start, its dedicated spinlock, PWM4 setup, timer alarm0/IRQ0, output-override release and cutoff. No confirmed activation-path root cause has been identified. Host boundary tests passed earlier but do not exercise the real SMP/IRQ/USB/power interaction.

The next experiment is the distinctly named v0.3p NO_DRIVE probe, keeping both motor inputs in coast while retaining the same PWM/timer path. This isolates release/physical activation effects without rewiring or increasing power/duty. It is not a root-cause correction. See docs/MOTOR_PROBE_V0_3P.md for the one-press procedure and limits of both possible outcomes. No probe board result is claimed yet.
