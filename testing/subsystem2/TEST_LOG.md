Legacy polling and both review defects have expected failing assertion logs dated 2026-11-06; none are claimed as on-board executions.

v0.3 adds test_motor_control.c and test_motor_pulse.c. The real driver is exercised against reset-aware register/kernel boundary fakes, checking every output operation for intended polarity/coast and unchanged ADC muxes, independent IRQ cutoff without task execution, wrap/caps/conflicts, expired setup, PWM reset release/timeout and validation/preparation of the shared microsecond timebase without resetting its counter. The controller covers fresh presses, held/bouncing buttons, STOP/fault precedence, all four steps, cooldown, wrap and the 500 ms fault fallback. All six host executables and normal/TEST builds pass in logs/2026-11-06-v0_3-final-build-result.json. Host fakes do not substitute for actual motor current, physical polarity or IRQ-latency measurements.
